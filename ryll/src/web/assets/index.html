No video: this browser offered no H.264

ryll streams H.264 and nothing else, and this browser did not offer it, so there is no video codec in common and nothing to show. Audio, keyboard, mouse and the cursor are unaffected and still work.

In Firefox, check that the OpenH264 plugin is installed and enabled under about:addons → Plugins. Chromium and Chrome ship H.264 and do not have this problem.
